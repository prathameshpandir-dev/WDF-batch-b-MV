🏛️ About The Backbenchers University

Established 1998

Founded in 1998, The Backbenchers University (BBU) is recognized as one of Maharashtra's premier autonomous engineering institutions. With an emphasis on practical problem solving, industry collaboration, and open-source contributions, BBU transforms inquisitive learners into global technology innovators.

Vision

To be a globally recognized center of excellence in engineering education, pioneering impactful research and nurturing ethical engineers.

Mission

To deliver rigorous, project-oriented computing curricula, state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure, and robust industry-academia linkages.

Core Values

Integrity, critical inquiry, teamwork, environmental sustainability, and dedication to societal upliftment through technology.

🔬 Specialized Engineering Laboratories

1. High Performance Cloud & GPU Computing Lab

Equipped with 4x NVIDIA A100 GPU servers for training deep learning models, rendering complex simulations, and academic research in LLMs.

2. IoT & Embedded Robotics Testbed

Features Raspberry Pi 5 clusters, ESP32 boards, LoRaWAN gateways, 3D printers, and digital oscilloscopes for hardware interfacing projects.

3. Cyber Security & Digital Forensics Lab

Air-gapped sandboxes for malware analysis, penetration testing simulations, cryptography research, and ethical hacking competitions.

4. Software Engineering & Full-Stack Studio

Collaborative scrum spaces with dual monitors, CI/CD automated test runners, Docker containerization nodes, and code review displays.
